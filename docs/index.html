About

One person, on purpose

Why all three crafts sit with the same pair of hands.

I started in 2021 cutting video and running accounts, picked up the web side after that, and got tired of watching brands pay three freelancers to produce three different personalities.

So I work the other way round. The same person who cuts your campaign builds the page it points at and writes the captions underneath it. Nothing gets lost in the handover, because there is no handover.

Most of my work starts with footage or an account that already exists and has never quite landed. That is the part I like: the material is usually there, it just needs someone to decide what it is saying.

Questions

Answered before you ask

The things clients want settled before any money changes hands.

How many revision rounds do I get?

Revisions are agreed up front and written into the quote, so you know the number before you commit rather than after. They are proper rounds too, not one note each.

Who owns the files at the end?

You do, in full, once the final invoice clears — project files and source assets, not just the exports. I keep an archive copy for a while afterwards in case you need a recut.

Can you work with footage I already have?

That is most of what I do. Send a link to the folder and I will tell you honestly whether there is a film in there before you pay anything.

What do you need from me to start a site build?

Copy, or a willingness to let me write it. Logo files, brand fonts if you have them, and one person with the authority to approve things. Missing copy is what delays builds, never the code.

Do you handle paid ads as well as organic?

I build and cut the creative, and I will set up the campaign structure. I do not manage ad spend day to day; for that I can point you at someone who does.

How do payments work?

A deposit books the dates and the balance falls due on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance. The split and the notice period are both set out in the quote before anything starts.
